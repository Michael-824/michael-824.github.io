FRC · 2023

Forklift Project

Design Question

How could the forklift prongs fit within the full robot assembly, and what gearbox reduction would provide enough torque at the cam to operate the mechanism?

Design Overview

The full-robot CAD shows the two forklift prongs in relation to the chassis and lifting structure. The gearbox calculations connect that geometry to the motor choice: a 152 lbf design load acting 35 in from the pivot produces a moment of about 443.3 ft·lbf. The spreadsheet translates that load through the mechanism geometry to a target cam torque of 57.37 ft·lbf.

Gearbox Calculations

The four stages use driving-to-driven tooth counts of 14:70, 20:70, 30:30, and 18:36. Together they give a 35:1 reduction. Using the spreadsheet’s motor torque input of 2.06 ft·lbf, the ideal gearbox output is 72.1 ft·lbf before drivetrain losses. That puts the calculated output about 14.7 ft·lbf above the cam torque target.

My Contribution

I helped assemble the physical forklift mechanism on the robot. The images follow the project from the prong layout in CAD and the gearbox sizing calculations to the hands-on assembly.
